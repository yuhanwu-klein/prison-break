🎬 Video Background Removal Demo

Transparent video over custom background

About This Demo

This page demonstrates video background removal using OpenCV's motion-based background subtraction algorithm.

Technical Details

  • Original Video: 720x976 resolution, 24fps, 121 frames
  • Processing Method: MOG2 Background Subtractor (OpenCV)
  • Output Formats:
    • WebM (VP9 codec with alpha channel) - 1.6MB
    • MOV (PNG codec with RGBA) - 61MB
  • Background Image: WechatIMG561.jpg

How It Works

The transparent video is layered on top of the background image using CSS positioning. The video maintains its alpha channel, allowing the background to show through the removed areas.
